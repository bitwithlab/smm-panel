04 · Questions

The parts people ask about.

Is this a bot that fakes likes and comments?

No. EngageFlow organizes the response workflow for your own team and the pages you manage — it does not fabricate engagement on accounts you don't control. We connect via official Meta and X APIs where possible.

Which platforms are supported?

Facebook (pages and profiles you manage), Instagram (business and creator accounts), and X / Twitter. TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and LinkedIn modules are on the Phase 3 roadmap.

How does billing work in Bangladesh?

We accept bKash, Nagad, Rocket, and international cards via SSLCommerz. Invoices in BDT or USD, annual plans get a 15% discount, and there's a seven-day free trial with no card up front.

Can agencies resell under their own brand?

Yes. The Agency plan includes white-label domain, logo, PDF report branding, sub-account provisioning, and reseller pricing you control.

What's your compliance posture?

Per-account rate limits, queue-based scheduling, official APIs where available, and a human review checkpoint on every scheduled reply. We publish acceptable-use guidelines and ban accounts that attempt inauthentic behavior.
